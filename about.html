About

Self-taught developer from Egypt. I make small utility tools and website templates. Currently learning C++.

Background

I got into programming on my own. No bootcamp, no CS degree, just a laptop and a lot of broken scripts. Most of what I know came from reading docs, rebuilding things I liked, and asking a lot of questions.

What I make

Small, focused tools and templates. Things that solve one problem cleanly instead of trying to do everything. I'd rather ship something simple and useful than something impressive and unfinished.

What's next

Getting comfortable with C++, then putting together a few finished projects worth showing on the Projects page.
